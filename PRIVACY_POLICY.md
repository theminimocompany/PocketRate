# PocketRate Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PocketRate ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

This policy is designed to comply with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) / California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) where applicable. We may update it to address additional local requirements as our app becomes available in more regions.

## The short version

PocketRate is designed so that your financial data stays on your device. We have no accounts, no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no servers of our own. The only third parties that receive any information are the exchange-rate providers (to fetch rates) and Google AdMob (to show ads, subject to your consent).

## Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly from you. PocketRate does not require you to create an account, and we do not store your travel or expense data on our servers — we don't operate any.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device using Android's built-in storage:

- Trip details, expenses, companions, and cost splits you enter
- Cached exchange rates, so the converter works offline
- App settings (for example, your default currency and theme)
- Ad-related preferences (your consent choice and ad-free status)

**Device backups:** if you have Android's automatic backup feature enabled, this app data may be included in the encrypted backup stored in *your own* Google Drive account, so your trips survive moving to a new phone. This backup is managed entirely by Android and Google under your device settings (Settings → Google → Backup); we never have access to it. You can turn it off at any time in your device settings.

**Exports:** when you choose to export a trip, the app generates a CSV or PDF file locally on your device and hands it to Android's share sheet. The file is only shared with the app or person you pick — we never see it.

## Categories of personal information

Under CCPA/CPRA, the categories of personal information that may be processed through the app include:

- Identifiers (such as device advertising ID and IP address)
- Internet or network activity information
- Inferences drawn for advertising purposes

## Third-party services

### Currency data providers

To provide exchange rates, the app connects to public currency APIs (ExchangeRate-API and Frankfurter). These requests are made over HTTPS and contain no personal information beyond what any HTTPS request reveals (such as your IP address, which these services may log, and the currencies you request). Their use is governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control the accuracy, availability, or timeliness of the data these services provide.

### Advertising

PocketRate uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and other data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region requiring consent, the app asks for your consent before any advertising identifier is used, and you can withdraw that consent at any time in the app settings.

Google may use this information to provide, maintain, and improve its advertising services. For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information:** We do not sell your personal information. However, the use of certain advertising identifiers and related technologies may be considered "sharing" or "selling" under California law. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

## How we use information

We and our service providers use information to:

- Provide currency conversion and exchange-rate data
- Display advertisements
- Maintain and improve app functionality
- Ensure security and prevent fraud

## Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Consent** for personalized advertising and the use of advertising identifiers
- **Legitimate interests** for serving non-personalized ads, app security, and fraud prevention
- **Contractual necessity / legitimate interests** for fetching exchange rates necessary to operate the app

## Your privacy choices

**Personalized advertising:** You can opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device settings:
- Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
- Or visit https://adssettings.google.com

**Reset advertising identifier:** You can reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

**Consent management:** If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or another region requiring consent, we obtain your consent before using your advertising identifier. You can review or withdraw your consent at any time in the app settings.

**Do Not Track signals:** PocketRate does not respond to browser-based "Do Not Track" signals because it is a mobile application.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, delete, or object to its processing.

Because PocketRate stores your travel and expense data only on your device (and in your own Google account's device backup, if you enable it), we do not hold copies of that data on our servers. You can delete individual trips and expenses in the app, or delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob, please contact those services directly or adjust your device settings.

## Data retention

We do not retain personal information on our own servers. Data stored locally on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Third-party services such as Google AdMob may retain data according to their own policies.

## Data transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, by the third-party services described above. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

## Children's privacy

PocketRate is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app, including storing your data only on your device and communicating with third-party services over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
